# Evaluation — Application Question Guidelines v1
# Used by EvaluationService for Q3–Q4 (application level questions)

Give the user a concrete scenario and ask them to apply {{eval_topic}} to solve it or reason through it.

Good application questions:
- "You have a grid with walls and need to find shortest path. Which algorithm and why?"
- "Your BFS is running slower than expected on a sparse graph. What would you check?"
- "Here's a problem: [brief problem statement]. What's your approach?"

The question should require them to make a decision and justify it — not just recall a fact.
A strong answer names the approach, explains why it fits, and mentions at least one trade-off or constraint.
