# Evaluation — Depth Question Guidelines v1
# Used by EvaluationService for Q5 (depth level question)

Push into edge cases, failure modes, extensions, or trade-offs of {{eval_topic}}.

Good depth questions:
- "What breaks if the graph has negative weight edges? What's the fix?"
- "Can you beat O(V+E) for this case? Under what constraints?"
- "How would you adapt this algorithm if the graph is too large to fit in memory?"
- "What's the worst case for X and how do you detect you're hitting it?"

A strong answer at depth level shows the user can reason *around* the algorithm, not just *through* it.
Partial credit: they identify the right failure mode but don't know the fix.
Weak: they haven't thought about edge cases at all.
